# Stroke Care

Stroke Care is an AI-powered mobile application designed to support stroke patients during their rehabilitation journey. Recovering from a stroke often requires regular exercise, motivation, and continuous monitoring. This app helps patients follow their rehabilitation plan, track their daily progress, receive medication reminders, and access an AI assistant for guidance and encouragement. The goal is to make recovery easier, more organized, and more engaging while supporting both patients and caregivers.

The application begins with a secure user registration and login system. After signing in, users create a patient profile by providing basic information such as age, recovery stage, mobility level, affected side, and daily exercise goals. This information allows the app to personalize rehabilitation plans according to the patient's needs.

Stroke Care includes a daily rehabilitation plan that organizes exercises into morning, afternoon, and evening sessions. Each exercise includes a title, description, duration, and supporting images or videos to help patients understand how to perform it correctly. The exercise library contains different categories such as arm exercises, leg exercises, balance training, speech therapy, and hand movement exercises.

Patients can mark exercises as completed after finishing each session. The app records this information and displays progress through daily, weekly, and monthly summaries. This allows users to monitor their recovery and stay motivated by seeing their improvements over time.

Another important feature is the medication reminder system. Users can add their medications, set reminder times, and mark each medicine as taken. These reminders help patients follow their prescribed treatment schedule consistently.

The main feature of Stroke Care is the AI Rehabilitation Coach. Patients can ask questions about their recovery, such as difficulties with exercises, muscle stiffness, weakness, or motivation. The AI provides clear, supportive, and easy-to-understand responses. It explains exercises in simple language, encourages patients to continue their rehabilitation, and suggests safe modifications when appropriate. The AI never diagnoses diseases or prescribes medication. If serious or unusual symptoms are described, it advises the user to contact a healthcare professional immediately.

The app also generates daily motivational messages based on the user's progress. These personalized messages encourage patients to remain consistent with their rehabilitation and celebrate their achievements.

A caregiver dashboard allows family members or caregivers to monitor exercise completion, medication status, and overall recovery progress. This helps caregivers stay informed and provide additional support when needed.

Stroke Care is built using modern technologies including Flutter or React Native for mobile development, Firebase Authentication for secure login, Cloud Firestore for data storage, Firebase Storage for media files, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, and OpenAI GPT or Google Gemini for AI-powered assistance.

Overall, Stroke Care combines rehabilitation management, progress tracking, medication reminders, and artificial intelligence into a single mobile application. It is designed to improve patient engagement, encourage consistent rehabilitation, and provide accessible support throughout the recovery process. While the AI assistant offers helpful guidance and motivation, the application is intended to complement professional healthcare rather than replace medical advice or treatment.
